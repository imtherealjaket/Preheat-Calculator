Important

This calculator provides planning estimates only. Actual heating performance depends on equipment, environment, torch condition, fuel quality, operator technique, heat distribution, and heat losses.

Heating time is calculated from each compatible nozzle's source-rated BTU/hr value. The app does not apply an invented generic fuel multiplier.

Recommendations come from the original calculator and do not verify fuel compatibility, equipment compatibility, supply capacity, operating pressure, flashback protection, safe operating limits, or procedure requirements.

Verify all ratings against current manufacturer documentation and follow applicable welding procedures and site safety requirements.
